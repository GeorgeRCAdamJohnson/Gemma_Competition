# PRE-REGISTRATION — A20 fix-shape bucket arm, 2026-10-24

Written BEFORE the first run. Nothing below may be edited after the sweep starts.

## Question
Does feeding the model a leave-one-out table of fix-shape examples (derived from the
129 gold patches) improve E4B's resolve rate on rich_2725?

## Why this arm (evidence)
- The bug-shape taxonomy was built (probe_mistakes.py) and PASSED its pre-registered bar:
  top-10 shapes cover 119/129 = 92.2% (bar was >=50% = tractable).
- It was NEVER fed to any prompt. Zero prompts in the workspace contain fix-pattern content.
- Three prompt mechanisms (A18 worked-example, D2 structure, A19 short-anchor) all nulled
  on resolves. The binding constraint is WRONG CONTENT (61.2% of failures), not format.
- rich_2725 is the discriminating task: A3 resolves 6/18 = 33.3% (pinned).
  rich_2943 is 0/18 for every arm — excluded.

## Arm
A20 = A3 with the strategy paragraph (line 29) REPLACED by the same text plus a compact
fix-shape table with 6 concrete examples. Single-span substitution, verified byte-identical
elsewhere. +550 chars (+7.2%). Examples are leave-one-out: no rich_2725 or rich_2943 gold
content. sha256[:12] = 516b2f0e4acf.

## Design
- Cohort: rich_2725 only (the discriminating task).
- n: 18 reps per arm (A3 control + A20).
- 2 arms x 18 reps x 1 task = 36 runs.
- Serial (--conc 1), --isolate, same budgets as the shipped config.
- Arms interleaved by rep (sweep.py default).

## PRIMARY metric
Pinned resolve rate (rescore_pinned.py), A3 vs A20, Fisher exact two-sided, alpha 0.05.

## SECONDARY (reported, not decision)
- Per-run edit_file failure rate (run-level permutation).
- Landed edits, fresh-anchor success, wall time.

## GUARDS
1. Corruption rate must not rise above A3's.
2. Landed edits must not decline.
3. Prompt must not displace A3's delimiter instruction (verify byte-identity of that section).

## Known hazards
- gate_run resolve flags are unusable (driver_exception); ALL resolve numbers come from
  rescore_pinned.py.
- n=18 on a 33% base detects only large effects (~2x). A null does not falsify the idea.
- The examples are from fastapi/rich/requests tasks — transfer to rich_2725 is unproven.
